### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a motivated **Computer Science & Technology undergraduate** and **Full-Stack Developer** passionate about building scalable, modern, and user-focused software solutions. I specialize in **React, Spring Boot, and MERN**, with hands-on experience in **AWS Cloud**, **Docker**, and **PostgreSQL**.

I enjoy designing clean UI/UX, developing full-stack applications, and solving real-world problems through technology. Currently working on full-stack projects and advancing my knowledge in **machine learning**, especially anomaly detection and image-based wildfire prediction.

* 🌍 Based in **Tangalle, Sri Lanka**
* ✉️ Contact me at [maheeshanethmina@gmail.com](mailto:maheeshanethmina@gmail.com)
* 🧠 Currently learning **AWS Cloud, Docker, and Advanced Full-Stack Architecture**
* 🤝 Open to collaborating on **React, MERN, or Spring Boot** projects.
